      Video Downloader & Splitter

      Downloads a remote video (YouTube, Vimeo, ...) via yt-dlp and splits it into chunks up to 100MB for GitHub.

      Download All scans every work page for remote videos (YouTube/Vimeo previews and body embeds) that don't already have a local videos/uploads/ folder, downloads and splits those into chunks. The custom player replaces them at runtime. Review, then click Save Files to apply.

      Foto Thumbnails
      
                        
      Scans fotografie_selectie.html and generates compressed low-res JPEG thumbnails (max 480px, quality 60, progressive) under opdrachten/Fotografie/thumbs/. Rewrites the page so the grid shows thumbs first with data-full pointing to the high-res originals.
      
                        
      
                            
                        
      
                        
      Ready.
      
                        
      
                    
      

                    
      
                        
      Index Preview Compression
      
                        
      Regenerates ultra-light preview_360p.mp4 files for all works on the index, taken from the original video. Videos are trimmed to 30 seconds, encoded at 24fps with compression (CRF 30, capped at 1600kbps) and scaled down (max 960px) for very fast streaming. Missing sources are rebuilt from the chunked files. Results stored in videos/uploads/*/preview_360p.mp4.
      
                        
      
                            
                        
      
                        
      Ready.
      
                        
      
                    
      

                    
      
                        
      Full-Length Seek Preview Backfill
      
                        
      Generates the low-bitrate, full-length preview_full-*.mp4 proxy for any multi-chunk work that doesn't have one yet - the fallback the player seeks into when the streaming Service Worker isn't available, instead of downloading every raw chunk in full. Missing sources are rebuilt from the chunked files. Results stored in videos/uploads/*/preview_full-*.mp4.
